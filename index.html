n8n Template Lead Gen

Capture leads from any form to HubSpot and notify your team in Slack

Who it's for:

Marketers, founders, and sales teams collecting leads via website forms needing instant HubSpot + Slack sync.

What it does:

Replaces classic 3-step Zapier zaps (Form → CRM → Slack) at zero per-task execution cost.

Capture leads from any form to HubSpot and notify your team in Slack

  1. 1Webhook: Receives lead data (name, email, message) as a POST request from any form tool.
  2. 2Set Node: Normalizes and formats input fields.
  3. 3HubSpot Node: Creates or updates the contact record.
  4. 4Slack Node: Posts instant notification alert to designated team channels.
  5. 5Webhook Response: Returns a 200 HTTP response to confirm lead receipt.
n8n + OpenAI AI Pipeline

Generate and send personalized client proposals by client type via email with AI

Who it's for:

Sales teams, agencies, and freelancers sending 10+ proposals/week needing branded, tier-customized output.

What it does:

Automates proposal generation: validates input, classifies budgets, applies dynamic pricing, drafts AI copy, and emails branded HTML docs.

Generate & Send AI Proposals Workflow Execution

  1. 1. Submission: Client submits request via webhook POST from CRM/form or via hourly scheduler polling.
  2. 2. Normalization: Field name variants normalized into a consistent JSON schema.
  3. 3. Validation & Tiering: JS validates required fields & classifies budget into tier (starter / growth / enterprise / enterprise+).
  4. 4. Error Handling: Invalid requests trigger a polite error email detailing missing fields.
  5. 5. Routing: Switch node routes valid requests to 1 of 5 client templates (startup, enterprise, agency, nonprofit, standard).
  6. 6. Pricing Logic: Applies base prices, line items, and dynamic client discounts.
  7. 7. AI Copywriting: AI generates a personalized 7-section proposal utilizing client context & pricing data.
  8. 8. Formatting & Delivery: JS formats output into branded HTML email sent via SendGrid, logged to Google Sheets, and alerted to Slack.

n8n + Gemini Content Automation

Trivia Generation and Facebook Posting with Telegram Approval

Who it's for:

Social media managers and page owners who want automated content curation without sacrificing manual quality control, plus automation builders connecting chat apps, generative AI, and publishing platforms.

What it does:

Generates a unique trivia fact with Google Gemini, turns it into branded card art via Google Slides, routes it through a Telegram approval gate, and auto-publishes the approved post to a Facebook Page.

Trivia Generation and Facebook Posting with Telegram Approval

  1. 1Trigger Phase: A message or callback query from Telegram activates the workflow.
  2. 2AI Processing: On a trivia command, the Google Gemini node crafts a strict JSON-formatted fact, which is parsed and paired with a randomly chosen template design.
  3. 3Asset Generation: The workflow manipulates Google Slides to insert text, takes a thumbnail screenshot of the slide, downloads it, and re-uploads it to Google Drive as a publicly accessible image.
  4. 4Human-in-the-Loop Gate: Details are logged in a Google Sheets tracker as "Pending" while a photo preview with Approve/Reject inline buttons is sent to Telegram.
  5. 5Execution & Feedback: Approve updates the spreadsheet row and posts the content directly to Facebook with an admin confirmation; Reject logs the rejection and halts publishing.
n8n + Gemini Trading Signals

Telegram Crypto Analysis Bot

Who it's for:

Crypto traders looking for structured, data-driven technical analysis, and automation enthusiasts merging API data pipelines with AI-driven analytics and chat notifications.

What it does:

Pulls 1D/4H kline data, tickers, and order book depth from MEXC, scores each asset 1–10 with Gemini against trading rules, and delivers a BUY/SELL/HOLD alert with entry, stop-loss, and take-profit levels to Telegram while logging results to Google Sheets.

Telegram Crypto Analysis Bot

  1. 1Trigger & Routing: A Telegram message provides the asset symbol and timeframe, which is routed by market type (Spot vs. Futures).
  2. 2Data Fetching & Processing: Requests kline, ticker, and order book data from MEXC in parallel, handles missing records, and computes RSI, EMA, MACD, and Bollinger Bands via JavaScript code nodes.
  3. 3Evaluation Pipeline: Compiled indicators are passed to an AI analysis node that checks volume ratios, evaluates signals, and returns a structured JSON verdict.
  4. 4Output Delivery: Formats the result into a readable HTML message sent back to the Telegram user.
  5. 5Data Logging: Trade signals and technical outputs are recorded into a Google Sheets watchlist.
n8n + ElevenLabs Lead Qualification

Facebook Lead Qualification with AI Voice Calling

Who it's for:

Sales and growth teams that want instant voice follow-up on inbound leads, business owners and agencies running Facebook Lead Ads, and operations engineers orchestrating Supabase, ElevenLabs, Slack, and Gmail together.

What it does:

Captures new Facebook Lead Ads submissions, stores them in Supabase, triggers an outbound AI phone call via ElevenLabs, then updates the lead record with the call outcome and routes booked/not-booked notifications to Slack and Gmail.

Facebook Lead Qualification with AI Voice Calling

  1. 1Trigger: Listens for new form submissions from Facebook Lead Ads.
  2. 2Formatting & Storage: Cleans up the lead payload and inserts a new record into Supabase with a "new" status.
  3. 3AI Call Trigger: Places an outbound call via the ElevenLabs conversational AI API with personalized details, then updates Supabase with the active call tracking info.
  4. 4Webhook Handling: Listens for the ElevenLabs call completion event and parses transcripts, duration, summary, and qualification metrics.
  5. 5Database Update: Writes final call outcomes back to the lead's Supabase record.
  6. 6Conditional Routing: Branches on whether an appointment was booked, sending tailored Slack and Gmail alerts with full transcripts and summaries either way.
n8n + OpenRouter Community Management

Auto Reply to Instagram & Facebook Ad Comments with Lead Capture

Who it's for:

Digital agencies and marketers running service ads on Facebook and Instagram who want to scale community management, and social media managers who need automated brand-safety alerting.

What it does:

Classifies incoming ad comments into query, positive review, negative review, or spam using an AI agent, drafts context-aware replies from a knowledge base for queries and positive reviews, and flags negative reviews to Slack for manual intervention.

Auto Reply to Instagram & Facebook Ad Comments with Lead Capture

  1. 1Trigger: A webhook fires whenever a new comment is posted on an Instagram or Facebook ad.
  2. 2Filtering & Retrieval: Validates the incoming payload, then fetches full comment content and parent ad metadata such as captions and permalinks.
  3. 3Cleaning & Classification: Cleaned data is sent to an AI comment classifier (OpenRouter) that determines intent and confidence.
  4. 4Routing: Queries and positive reviews go to a Comment Generator agent, which drafts a reply using a Google Docs knowledge base when needed and posts it via the Facebook API.
  5. 5Escalation: Negative reviews skip automated replying and trigger a Slack alert to the team for manual review.
