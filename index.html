Featured Work

Explore interactive automated workflows built across key integration platforms.

n8n Template Lead Gen

Capture leads from any form to HubSpot and notify your team in Slack

Who it's for:

Marketers, founders, and sales teams collecting leads via website forms needing instant HubSpot + Slack sync.

What it does:

Replaces classic 3-step Zapier zaps (Form → CRM → Slack) at zero per-task execution cost.

Capture leads from any form to HubSpot and notify your team in Slack

  1. 1Webhook: Receives lead data (name, email, message) as a POST request from any form tool.
  2. 2Set Node: Normalizes and formats input fields.
  3. 3HubSpot Node: Creates or updates the contact record.
  4. 4Slack Node: Posts instant notification alert to designated team channels.
  5. 5Webhook Response: Returns a 200 HTTP response to confirm lead receipt.
n8n + OpenAI AI Pipeline

Generate and send personalized client proposals by client type via email with AI

Who it's for:

Sales teams, agencies, and freelancers sending 10+ proposals/week needing branded, tier-customized output.

What it does:

Automates proposal generation: validates input, classifies budgets, applies dynamic pricing, drafts AI copy, and emails branded HTML docs.

Generate & Send AI Proposals Workflow Execution

  1. 1. Submission: Client submits request via webhook POST from CRM/form or via hourly scheduler polling.
  2. 2. Normalization: Field name variants normalized into a consistent JSON schema.
  3. 3. Validation & Tiering: JS validates required fields & classifies budget into tier (starter / growth / enterprise / enterprise+).
  4. 4. Error Handling: Invalid requests trigger a polite error email detailing missing fields.
  5. 5. Routing: Switch node routes valid requests to 1 of 5 client templates (startup, enterprise, agency, nonprofit, standard).
  6. 6. Pricing Logic: Applies base prices, line items, and dynamic client discounts.
  7. 7. AI Copywriting: AI generates a personalized 7-section proposal utilizing client context & pricing data.
  8. 8. Formatting & Delivery: JS formats output into branded HTML email sent via SendGrid, logged to Google Sheets, and alerted to Slack.
n8n + Claude Analytics Digest

Daily Outbound Performance Digest

Who it's for:

Outbound teams wanting a concise daily morning digest on campaign performance without manually sifting spreadsheets.

What it does:

Reads campaign sheets, analyzes sentiment using Claude, ranks follow-ups, and lands a concise digest directly in your inbox.

Daily Outbound Digest Execution

  1. 1Schedule Trigger: Fires automatically once every morning.
  2. 2Google Sheets Node: Reads active outbound campaign rows.
  3. 3Aggregate Node: Combines spreadsheet rows into a single payload.
  4. 4Claude Agent: Analyzes data and returns summary, sentiment breakdown, and ranked priority follow-ups.
  5. 5Gmail Node: Sends formatted email digest to user inbox.
